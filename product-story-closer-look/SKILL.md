Build a `Take a closer look` style section that turns the product object into the centerpiece.

## Mandatory Preparation

Invoke `product-story-design` first. If context is missing, run `teach-product-story`.

Read `../product-story-design/references/component-patterns.md` and `../product-story-design/references/product-page-patterns.md`.

## Purpose

This section exists to slow the page down and let users study the object itself.

Use it when:

- industrial design matters
- finishes or colors matter
- the silhouette or materials are a core reason to buy

## Structure

Typical pieces:

- short section title, often `Take a closer look.`
- large central render or layered product viewer
- color or finish controls if relevant
- minimal support copy
- optional CTA such as `View in your space`

## Design Rules

- Let the object be the interface.
- Reduce surrounding UI chrome.
- Keep text sparse and secondary.
- Use scale, lighting, and background restraint to make the product feel premium.
- Make interaction feel tactile but not flashy.

## Motion Rules

- Slow reveals and smooth transitions are appropriate.
- Color or finish changes should feel immediate and elegant.
- Avoid carousel-like gimmick behavior unless genuinely useful.

## Best Fit

Strongest for:

- flagship launches
- creator devices with strong hardware identity
- sensory products with iconic industrial design

Usually unnecessary for:

- value launches with weaker design differentiation
- compatibility-led accessory pages
- short co-branded shopping pages

## Quality Check

Before finishing, verify:

- the product feels studied, not merely displayed
- the section adds desire, not just information
- the product remains beautiful on mobile
