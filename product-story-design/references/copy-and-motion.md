# Copy And Motion

## Copy Principles

### Headlines

Headlines should be:

- short
- clean
- emotionally controlled
- easy to scan

Good shapes:

- noun + promise
- product name + concise benefit
- a plainspoken line with strong cadence

Avoid:

- stacked buzzwords
- filler intros
- vague innovation language
- copy that explains too much too early

### Support Copy

Support copy should clarify the headline in one or two sentences.

Prefer:

- one concrete benefit
- one sentence of proof or context
- simple punctuation and rhythm

### CTA Logic

Premium product-story pages often use paired CTAs:

- `Learn more` for exploration
- `Buy`, `Shop`, or `Pre-order` for action

If there is no commerce action, pair exploration with another lightweight path such as `Watch the film`, `See it in AR`, or `Compare models`.

Do not invent too many CTA variants on one page.

## Motion Principles

### Purpose

Motion should do one of these jobs:

- reveal hierarchy
- direct attention
- create continuity between sections
- suggest product depth or responsiveness

If it does not serve one of those jobs, cut it.

### Timing

- Entrances should feel smooth and slightly delayed, not snappy by default.
- Large visuals can move slower than text.
- Hover states should be restrained and immediate.

### Preferred Behaviors

- fade + translate
- scale with restraint
- masked reveal
- parallax with very low amplitude
- sticky transitions when they clarify narrative flow

### Avoid

- bounce
- elastic easing
- rotating decorative objects with no meaning
- continuous floating animations
- multiple simultaneous hero effects

## Voice Checklist

Before shipping, ask:

- Does the copy sound certain without sounding arrogant?
- Can each section be understood in a few seconds?
- Are there too many words competing with the imagery?
- Does motion make the product feel better, or just busier?
