# Wearable Product Patterns

Use leading smartwatch product pages as the reference for products sold through daily-life utility, readiness, and personal attachment.

Reviewed pages:

- [Apple Watch SE 3](https://www.apple.com/apple-watch-se-3/)
- [Apple Watch Ultra 3](https://www.apple.com/apple-watch-ultra-3/)
- [Apple Watch Nike](https://www.apple.com/apple-watch-nike/)

## Core Logic

The strongest wearable pages are not organized around components first. They are organized around life contexts:

- health
- fitness
- running
- safety
- outdoors
- style and band personalization

The device is sold as an always-there companion.

## Page Types Inside Watch Family

### Mainline Or Value Watch

Use the value smartwatch page as reference.

Traits:

- accessible value framing
- practical feature chapters
- more obvious buying clarity
- less visual indulgence than Ultra

### Rugged Or Extreme Watch

Use the rugged smartwatch page as reference.

Traits:

- durability and environment become part of the visual language
- product imagery feels more adventure-oriented
- proof clusters around endurance, precision, and readiness

### Collaboration Variant

Use the co-branded smartwatch page as reference.

Traits:

- shorter page
- stronger identity and apparel-adjacent styling
- quicker route to shopping

## Transferable Lessons

- For body-worn products, organize by moments of use.
- For fitness products, chapters can map to actual activity types.
- Finishes, bands, and personalization matter because the object is also fashion-adjacent.
- Utility and emotion must coexist.
