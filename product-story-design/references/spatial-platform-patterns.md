# Vision Pro Patterns

Use Vision Pro-like launches as the model for pages that introduce a new interface paradigm, computing platform, or worldview shift.

Reviewed page:

- [Apple Vision Pro](https://www.apple.com/apple-vision-pro/)

## Core Logic

Vision Pro is not just a hardware page. It behaves like a manifesto for a new category.

The page must do three jobs:

1. establish that the category matters
2. explain how the interaction model works
3. make the future feel tangible and desirable

## Structural Traits

- Hero feels declarative and historic.
- Copy is broken into short staged lines.
- Chapters often map to new behavior models, not classic feature categories.
- Visuals explain spatial interaction, work, media, communication, and memory in a new form.

## Writing Traits

- More visionary than typical hardware launch pages
- Still restrained, but with a stronger sense of category introduction
- Benefits are often framed as a new way to do something familiar

## Transferable Lessons

- If the product changes a user's mental model, teach the model first.
- When a product is hard to compare, frame it as a shift in experience rather than a checklist of specs.
- Use typography, pacing, and imagery to create inevitability, not hype.
