Perform a restrained, high-precision finishing pass. This is for making the work feel launch-ready, not for changing the core concept.

## Mandatory Preparation

Invoke `product-story-design` first. If design context is missing, run `teach-product-story`.

Only polish work that is already functionally complete.

## What To Refine

Review these dimensions:

- spacing consistency
- visual alignment
- CTA clarity
- copy length and cadence
- surface tint and shadow subtlety
- image crop quality
- hover, focus, active, loading, and reduced-motion behavior
- mobile composition fidelity

## Polish Standards

### Surfaces

- Remove heavy shadows and noisy effects.
- Use subtle separation, not obvious decoration.
- Ensure tinted neutrals feel deliberate and cohesive.

### Motion

- Keep transitions smooth and quiet.
- Use motion to support reveal and continuity.
- Eliminate any animation that feels ornamental.

### Copy

- Trim extra words from headings and support text.
- Keep CTA language consistent across the page.
- Remove repeated claims.

### Alignment

- Check optical alignment, not only geometric alignment.
- Confirm spacing tokens are used consistently.
- Make sure related content snaps into a visible rhythm.

### Images

- Refine crop, scale, and framing.
- Avoid awkward dead space around the main subject.
- Ensure imagery remains sharp and intentional on smaller screens.

## Final Check

Before finishing, verify:

- Nothing feels generic or templated.
- Nothing calls attention to itself without serving the product story.
- The page feels more edited than decorated.
- Mobile still feels premium, not compressed desktop.
