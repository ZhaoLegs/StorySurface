## Design Context

### Product

Lumen Buds Pro are premium wireless earbuds designed to make music, calls, and focus time feel immersive and effortless.

Core promise:

`Hear what matters. Lose what doesn't.`

Desired action:

Drive users toward `Buy` after building emotional conviction and practical trust.

### Audience

- commuters
- remote workers
- frequent flyers
- people upgrading from generic earbuds

Mindset:

They want proof of comfort, sound quality, and noise control before spending premium money.

### Brand Tone

- quiet
- precise
- immersive

Should not feel:

- gamer
- aggressive
- over-techy
- bargain-oriented

### Visual Direction

- dark and light versions both possible, but hero should lean dark for atmosphere
- object-led photography and close-up detail matter
- color palette should stay restrained: graphite, silver mist, midnight blue
- motion should feel fluid and close to tactile

### Design Principles

- Lead with feeling before technical explanation.
- Let industrial detail and close-up scale sell quality.
- Keep copy sparse and image-led.
- Translate invisible benefits with visual metaphors and precise proof.

## 5. Highlights Deliverable

If `product-story-highlights` were run alone, the section would use:

- one horizontal topic rail on desktop
- stacked cards without heavy borders on mobile
- one motion moment per highlight, never all at once

Editorial rule:

Every highlight sentence should still work if read alone.

## 6. Closer-Look Deliverable

If `product-story-closer-look` were run alone, it would prioritize:

- finish selection
- earbud contour
- case hinge quality
- material and vent details

This is a desire-building section, not a specs section.

## 7. Typeset Notes

- Display headings: short, high-contrast, tightly led
- Body width: capped for comfortable reading
- Avoid more than one visible font family
- Use copy edits aggressively before adding more visual decoration

## 8. Arrange Notes

- hero gets the most breathing room
- highlights are compact and preview-like
- feature chapters alternate dense proof and airy product imagery
- comparison section becomes tighter and quieter

## 9. Polish Notes

- ensure finish selector feels immediate
- trim any repeated claims across chapters
- keep shadows and glows barely noticeable
- verify mobile crop quality for earbuds and case

## 10. Audit Result

### Verdict

`Strong`

### What this test proves

- The skill system can choose an archetype correctly.
- It can derive a coherent full-page sequence.
- It can split the page into reusable modules cleanly.
- It can carry one visual language across hero, highlights, and deep dives.

### Gaps still exposed

- The system would benefit from a dedicated `product-story-compare` skill.
- The system would benefit from a dedicated `product-story-mobile-pass` skill.
- A reusable output template for real implementation handoff would make the flow even stronger.
