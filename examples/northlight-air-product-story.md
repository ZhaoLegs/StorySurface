## Design Context

### Product

Northlight Air is a lightweight laptop built for everyday creative and professional work, with a focus on portability, battery life, and display quality.

Core promise:

`Light enough to go anywhere. Strong enough to stay all day.`

Desired action:

Move users toward `Buy` after making the product feel easy, beautiful, and dependable.

### Audience

- students
- remote workers
- frequent commuters
- people upgrading from older thin laptops

Mindset:

They want a machine that feels refined and capable without the intimidation of a workstation product.

### Brand Tone

- calm
- bright
- capable

Should not feel:

- gamer
- corporate
- over-engineered
- benchmark-obsessed

### Visual Direction

- light-led palette with a more atmospheric dark hero acceptable
- color lineup matters
- product silhouette and edge profile matter
- motion should emphasize lift, thinness, and display quality

### Design Principles

- Lead with ease before hard proof.
- Use thinness and color as emotional entry points.
- Keep workflow proof practical and human-scaled.
- Let display content demonstrate the value of the device.

## 4. Validation Result

### Verdict

`Strong`

### What this proves

- The system can switch archetypes successfully.
- It can move from sensory-product storytelling to computing-product storytelling without reusing the same proof strategy.
- It can preserve the same design language while changing page logic.

### Remaining opportunities

- Add a dedicated `product-story-compare` skill.
- Add a `product-story-screen-content` reference for showing better in-device UI content.
