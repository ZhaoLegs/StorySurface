Lightweight computing pages work best when they sell movement and readiness first. Thinness is not just a spec. It is a behavioral promise.

Display

More room for what matters.

Screen value is sold through content curation. The in-device image should prove color, clarity, and workflow, not behave like generic placeholder UI.

  • Show believable work content
  • Use fewer but stronger screen moments
  • Make the screen part of the product story
